# PACIFY ELECTRONICS PRIVATE LIMITED
## Refunds Policy

**Document reference:** POL-REF-001
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective from:** 15 January 2026
**Owner:** Finance Operations

---

## S1. Scope

S1.1 This Policy governs the calculation and disbursement of refunds. It applies only once a return has been approved under the Returns Policy (POL-RET-002), or once a warranty claim has been resolved in favour of a refund under the Warranty Policy (POL-WAR-001).

S1.2 This Policy does not determine whether a customer is entitled to return an item. That question is governed exclusively by POL-RET-002.

S1.3 Where this Policy conflicts with the EU Regional Addendum (POL-EU-001) in respect of a customer resident in the European Union, the Addendum prevails.

---

## S2. Refund method

S2.1 Refunds are issued to the original payment instrument used for the purchase. Pacify does not issue refunds to an alternative instrument except in the circumstances set out at S9.

S2.2 Where an order was settled using more than one instrument, the refund is apportioned across those instruments in the same proportion as the original payment.

S2.3 Where an order was settled in whole or in part using store credit or a gift card, that portion is refunded as store credit and not as cash.

---

## S3. Processing periods

S3.1 The following periods apply from the date the return is approved under POL-RET-002 S11.3, or from the date the warranty resolution is confirmed.

| Original payment method | Refund destination | Processing period |
|---|---|---|
| UPI | Same UPI handle | 3-5 business days |
| Credit card | Same card | 5-7 business days |
| Debit card | Same card | 5-7 business days |
| Net banking | Originating account | 5-7 business days |
| EMI (card-based) | Card, principal only | 7-14 business days |
| Cash on delivery | Bank transfer, details required | 7-10 business days |
| Gift card / store credit | Store credit | Instant |
| Store credit (elective) | Store credit, plus 5% bonus | Instant |

S3.2 The periods at S3.1 measure the time taken by Pacify and by the receiving financial institution combined. Pacify initiates all refunds within 1 business day of approval; the remainder of the period is attributable to banking settlement and is outside Pacify's control.

S3.3 A refund is complete when the funds are credited by the receiving institution. A reference number is issued to the customer on initiation and may be quoted to the customer's bank.

S3.4 The periods at S3.1 are distinct from the reversal of a failed payment, which is governed by the Payments Policy (POL-PAY-001) S4 and which arises where no order was ever completed.

---

## S4. Calculation of the refund

S4.1 The refund payable is calculated as follows:

```
    item price actually paid
  - restocking fee              (POL-RET-002 S10)
  - return shipping charge      (POL-RET-002 S9)
  + original outbound shipping  (only where S4.3 applies)
  = refund payable
```

S4.2 "Item price actually paid" means the sum charged to the customer for the item after the application of any discount, coupon, promotional reduction, or no-cost EMI adjustment. It does not mean the published list price.

S4.3 The original outbound shipping charge is refunded only where the return arises from:

(a) an item damaged or defective on arrival (POL-RET-002 S6);
(b) delivery of an incorrect item (POL-RET-002 S7);
(c) a material discrepancy between the item and its published description.

In all other cases the original outbound shipping charge is not refunded.

S4.4 Worked example. A customer purchases a laptop for Rs 64,900 with free shipping, opens it, and returns it within 14 days having changed their mind:

| Line | Amount (Rs) |
|---|---|
| Item price actually paid | 64,900 |
| Less restocking fee at 10% | (6,490) |
| Less return shipping | (450) |
| Plus original outbound shipping | 0 |
| **Refund payable** | **57,960** |

S4.5 Worked example. The same laptop, returned within 48 hours because it arrived with a cracked lid, where the customer paid Rs 99 outbound shipping:

| Line | Amount (Rs) |
|---|---|
| Item price actually paid | 64,900 |
| Less restocking fee | 0 |
| Less return shipping | 0 |
| Plus original outbound shipping | 99 |
| **Refund payable** | **64,999** |

S4.6 Where the calculation at S4.1 produces a figure exceeding the amount originally charged, the excess is issued as store credit rather than as cash.

---

## S5. Partial refunds

S5.1 Where a multi-item order is partially returned, the refund is calculated per item under S4.

S5.2 Where free shipping was applied to the order by reason of the order total exceeding the threshold at POL-SHP-001 S3, and the partial return reduces the retained order value below that threshold, the shipping charge that would have applied is deducted from the refund.

S5.3 Where a discount was applied conditional on the purchase of multiple items, and the return breaks that condition, the discount is recalculated and the difference deducted.

---

## S6. Store credit alternative

S6.1 A customer entitled to a refund may elect to receive store credit instead.

S6.2 Store credit is issued instantly on approval of the return and carries a bonus of 5% of the refund value calculated under S4.

S6.3 Store credit is valid for 24 months from issue, is not transferable, and cannot be converted to cash. An election under S6.1 is irrevocable.

---

## S7. EMI refunds

S7.1 Where an order was financed by EMI, the refund returns the **principal amount only**.

S7.2 Interest, processing charges, and any other financing costs already levied by the issuing bank are **not refunded by Pacify** and are not recoverable from Pacify. The customer may pursue the issuing bank in respect of such charges, and Pacify will provide documentation in support on request.

S7.3 Where the EMI was advertised as "no-cost EMI", the interest was borne by Pacify in the form of a discount to the item price at the point of sale. On return, the refund is of the **discounted amount actually paid**, and not of the published list price. The customer does not receive the value of the absorbed interest.

S7.4 Worked example. A laptop listed at Rs 64,900 is purchased on no-cost EMI, with Rs 4,200 of interest absorbed by Pacify as a discount, such that the customer is charged Rs 60,700 in instalments. On return within the window, and assuming an opened item returned by choice:

| Line | Amount (Rs) |
|---|---|
| Item price actually paid | 60,700 |
| Less restocking fee at 10% | (6,070) |
| Less return shipping | (450) |
| **Refund payable** | **54,180** |

S7.5 The cancellation of the EMI mandate with the issuing bank may take a further 1 to 2 billing cycles. Instalments falling due in that period may still be debited and are subsequently reconciled by the bank.

---

## S8. Cash on delivery refunds

S8.1 Where an order was paid by cash on delivery, no payment instrument exists to which a refund can be issued. The customer must supply bank account details.

S8.2 Pacify requires the account holder name, account number, and IFSC code. The account holder name must correspond to the name on the order.

S8.3 The processing period at S3.1 for cash on delivery orders runs from the date complete and valid bank details are received, not from the date the return was approved.

S8.4 The cash on delivery handling fee levied under POL-PAY-001 S3 is not refundable.

---

## S9. Failed refunds

S9.1 A refund may fail where the original instrument has expired, been cancelled, been reported lost, or where the associated account has been closed.

S9.2 Where a refund fails, Pacify notifies the customer within 2 business days and requests alternative details. Provision of alternative details is subject to the identity verification requirements at POL-CS-001 S6.

S9.3 A refund that cannot be disbursed after two attempts and 60 calendar days is converted to store credit. Store credit issued under this subsection does not attract the bonus at S6.2.

---

## S10. Price drop protection

S10.1 Where the price of an item falls within 7 calendar days of delivery, the customer may claim the difference.

S10.2 A claim under S10.1 is paid as store credit or to the original instrument at the customer's election, and does not attract the bonus at S6.2.

S10.3 Price drop protection does not apply to prices offered during a flash sale, a clearance event, or a promotion restricted to a defined customer segment.

---

## S11. Tracking a refund

S11.1 Refund status is visible in the Orders section of the customer's Pacify account, and passes through the states: Approved, Initiated, In transit with bank, Credited.

S11.2 Where a refund shows as Initiated and the period at S3.1 has elapsed, the customer should raise the reference number with their bank in the first instance. Pacify cannot accelerate settlement once funds have left its account.

---

## S12. Disputes and chargebacks

S12.1 A customer who initiates a chargeback with their card issuer in respect of an order for which a refund is already in progress should notify Pacify, as parallel proceedings may delay both.

S12.2 Where a chargeback is raised and a refund has already been disbursed, Pacify will contest the chargeback and supply evidence of disbursement.

S12.3 Disputes as to the amount of a refund calculated under S4 are handled under the escalation procedure at POL-CS-001 S3.
