## S3. Setting up

S3.1 Connect the supplied adapter and charge for 30 minutes before first use.

S3.2 Press power once. First-time setup takes about 10 minutes.

S3.3 Install pending updates before using the machine in earnest.

---

## S4. Charging

S4.1 Use the supplied 65 W adapter. A lower-wattage charger will run the machine but will charge slowly or not at all under load.

S4.2 Either USB-C port accepts charge.

S4.3 Battery health is visible in Pacify Diagnostics. See S7.

---

## S5. Display and external monitors

S5.1 Both USB-C ports carry DisplayPort alternate mode. HDMI 2.0 supports 4K at 60 Hz.

S5.2 Maximum two external displays.

S5.3 Many USB-C cables carry power only. If an external display is not detected over USB-C, try the cable supplied with the monitor before assuming a fault.

## S7. Pacify Diagnostics

S7.1 Pre-installed. Reports battery capacity, storage health, memory integrity, fan behaviour, and thermal history.

S7.2 The maximum capacity figure is the one required for a battery warranty claim. Third-party readings are not accepted.

S7.3 Diagnostics reports codes in the form BAT-, THRM-, STO-, MEM-, KEY-, DSP-. See FAQ-TEC-001 S9.

---

## S8. Care and maintenance

S8.1 Clean the display with a dry microfibre cloth. Do not use solvents.

S8.2 Keep the underside vents clear. Operating on bedding causes thermal throttling and reports code THRM-88.

S8.3 Operating range 5 to 35 degrees Celsius. Charging pauses outside this range and reports BAT-007.

S8.4 For long storage, leave the battery at about 50% and power down fully.

---

## S9. Service

S9.1 The ProBook 14 has no user-serviceable internal components. Memory and storage are soldered.

S9.2 Opening the chassis does not void cover for unrelated faults, but damage caused in doing so is not covered.

S9.3 Raise warranty claims under POL-WAR-001 S10.
