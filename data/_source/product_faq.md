# Pacify — Frequently Asked Questions

**Document reference:** FAQ-PRD-001
**Last updated:** 15 January 2026

Everything customers ask us most, answered plainly. If you need the formal wording,
each answer points you to the policy it comes from.

## S1. Ordering and your account

**Do I need an account to buy something?**
Yes. You need an account so we can show you your order status, handle returns, and keep your invoices in one place.

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
You can change the delivery address any time before we hand the parcel to the courier. After that, we cannot redirect it. You cannot swap items on an existing order — cancel it and place a new one.

**Can I cancel an order?**
Yes, free of charge, right up until dispatch. After dispatch you will need to refuse the delivery or return the item.

**I never got my order confirmation email.**
Check your spam folder first. If it is not there, your order may not have gone through — look for it in your account. No order in your account means no order was created, and any money that left your bank comes back on its own within 5 to 7 working days.

**Can I place an order over the phone?**
No. All orders go through the website or the app.

**Do you keep my card details?**
Not the numbers, no. Saved cards are tokenised, which means the card network holds the real number and we only hold a token. Our staff will never ask you for a full card number, CVV, PIN, or OTP.

**Can I buy on behalf of my company?**
Yes. Add your GSTIN at checkout and the invoice will carry it. You cannot add a GSTIN after we have dispatched.

**What if I order 5 or more of the same thing?**
That counts as a bulk order and the return window is shorter — 7 days rather than 30. Worth knowing before you buy for an office.

## S2. Products and specifications

**Are your products genuine and new?**
Yes, unless the listing says "refurbished". Refurbished units are clearly marked and come with a 6-month warranty rather than the usual period.

**What is the difference between a Pacify-branded product and the other brands you sell?**
Two things. Pacify hardware carries 24 months of warranty rather than 12. And if something goes wrong with a Pacify product, we handle the whole repair ourselves. For other brands, the warranty is the manufacturer's and you deal with their service network — we can point you to the nearest centre and confirm your purchase date, but we cannot authorise a repair on their behalf.

**Do your laptops come with an operating system?**
Yes, pre-installed and activated.

**Can I upgrade the RAM or storage myself?**
On the ProBook 16 the storage is user-accessible. On the ProBook 14 and 14 Lite, memory and storage are soldered. Opening the chassis yourself will not void your hardware warranty for unrelated faults, but any damage you cause while doing it is not covered.

**Is the item in stock?**
The product page shows live availability. "Ships in 24 hours" means we have it. "Made to order" means the page will tell you the dispatch date.

**Do you sell extended warranty?**
Yes — PacifyCare+, which adds 12 or 24 months. You have to buy it within 30 days of your delivery. It extends the length of cover but does not widen what is covered, so it is not accidental damage insurance.

**Are chargers included?**
Yes, with every laptop, phone, and tablet. Monitors include a power cable and one display cable.

**What is in the box?**
The device, its charger, cables, and documentation. Anything else is listed on the product page.

**Do you ship internationally?**
We ship across India and to twelve EU countries. Nowhere else at the moment.

## S3. Shipping and delivery

**How much is delivery?**
Free on orders of Rs 5,000 or more. Below that it is Rs 99 standard, or Rs 299 for express.

**How long will it take?**
We hand your parcel to the courier within 24 hours. Then it is 3 to 7 working days for standard, or 1 to 2 for express. So a standard order placed Monday morning usually lands somewhere between Thursday and the following Wednesday.

**Can I choose a delivery date?**
Not for standard or express. Large monitors — 32 inches and up — come with white glove delivery, and we book a slot with you for those.

**Where is my order?**
Your tracking link is in your account and in the dispatch email. Courier tracking can lag reality by half a day. If nothing has moved for five working days, tell us and we will chase it.

**Nobody was home. What now?**
The courier tries three times on three consecutive working days. After that the parcel comes back to us and we refund you, minus the outbound shipping if you paid any.

**My delivery is late. Do I get anything?**
If we miss a committed delivery date by more than five working days, you can claim Rs 500 in store credit, or the shipping you paid, whichever is more. That does not apply if the address was wrong or nobody was available.

**Do you deliver on Sundays?**
No.

**Will I pay customs on an EU order?**
Yes — duties and VAT are payable by you on arrival. We declare the true value on the invoice.

## S4. Returns and refunds

**How long do I have to return my laptop?**
Two weeks from the day it arrives, if you have opened it. A month if the seal is still intact. Accessories get a month either way.

**What if I opened the box?**
Then you are on the 14-day clock, and there is a 10% restocking fee on laptops, phones, tablets, and monitors. Accessories have no fee.

**Wait — is it 14 days or 30 days?**
For an opened laptop, phone, tablet, or monitor it is 14 days. The 30-day figure applies to sealed items and to accessories. The formal wording is in the Returns Policy at S2.

**What if it arrived damaged?**
Tell us within 48 hours and send photos. Then there is no restocking fee, no return shipping cost, and we refund the original delivery charge too. You can have a replacement instead of a refund if you prefer.

**Do I pay to send it back?**
Rs 450 if you simply changed your mind, taken off your refund. Nothing if the item was faulty, damaged, or the wrong thing.

**How much will I actually get back?**
Take the price you paid, subtract the restocking fee if one applies, subtract Rs 450 if you are sending it back by choice, and add back the original delivery charge if the item was faulty. There is a worked example in the Refunds Policy at S4.

**How long until the money arrives?**
Depends how you paid. UPI is 3 to 5 working days. Cards and net banking are 5 to 7. EMI takes 7 to 14. Cash on delivery is 7 to 10 once you have given us your bank details. The clock starts when we finish inspecting the return, not when you post it.

**Can I have store credit instead?**
Yes, and we add 5% on top. It is instant. But it is a one-way door — you cannot change it back to cash later.

**I bought on no-cost EMI. What happens if I return it?**
You get back what you actually paid in instalments, not the ticket price. On a no-cost EMI deal we quietly discount the item to cover the bank's interest, so the amount you paid is lower than the listed price and that is the figure we refund.

**Will I get my EMI interest back?**
No. Interest your bank has already charged is between you and your bank. We refund the principal. We will give you paperwork if you want to take it up with them.

**Can I exchange rather than return?**
Yes, for a different size or colour of the same product, within your return window. It counts as a return plus a new purchase, so the same fees apply.

## S5. Warranty

**How long is the warranty?**
24 months on Pacify-branded laptops, phones, tablets, and monitors. 12 months on other brands. 6 months on accessories and on refurbished units.

**When does it start?**
The day it is delivered. Not the day you ordered, and not the day you first switch it on.

**My screen has a couple of dead pixels.**
We replace a panel at five or more dead or stuck pixels. Below five, the panel is within manufacturing tolerance and is not covered — that is an industry-wide standard and our agents cannot make an exception. Other display faults like backlight failure, flicker, or discolouration are covered whatever the pixel count.

**My battery is not what it was.**
If it drops below 80% of its rated capacity inside 12 months, that is covered. Beyond 12 months, or above 80%, that is normal wear and a paid replacement.

**I dropped it.**
Not covered, sorry. Physical damage and liquid damage are both excluded. We will quote you for a paid repair.

**How long does a repair take?**
7 to 14 working days once the item reaches the service centre. Longer if a part has to be imported, and we will tell you if that happens.

**Do I get a loaner?**
Not as a rule. Occasionally for Pacify-branded items where we expect a long turnaround.

**Can I sell it on with the warranty?**
Yes. The cover follows the item. The new owner needs the original invoice.

## S6. Payment

**What can I pay with?**
UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, EMI, cash on delivery, gift cards, and store credit.

**Is there EMI?**
On orders of Rs 15,000 and up, with tenures from 3 to 24 months depending on your bank.

**Can I pay cash on delivery?**
Up to Rs 50,000, with a Rs 50 handling fee. That fee is not refundable, even if you return the item.

**My payment failed but I was charged.**
Your bank will reverse it by itself within 5 to 7 working days. We never received the money, so there is nothing for us to send back — this is a bank reversal, not a refund. If there is no order in your account, this is what has happened.

**I was charged twice.**
Tell us and we will check the gateway records within two working days. If it is a genuine duplicate, the extra charge comes back within 7 to 10 working days.

**Where is my invoice?**
Emailed within 24 hours of dispatch, and downloadable from your account for seven years.

**The price dropped right after I bought it.**
If it drops within 7 days of your delivery, claim the difference. Flash sales and clearance events do not count.
