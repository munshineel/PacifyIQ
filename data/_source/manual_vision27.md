## S3. Assembly

S3.1 Lay the panel face down on the foam insert. Attach the column, then the base. Tighten by hand.

S3.2 Keep the packaging for at least 14 days. A return requires the original packaging under POL-RET-002 S4.1(d).

---

## S4. Connecting

S4.1 For 2560 x 1440 at 75 Hz, use the supplied DisplayPort cable. HDMI 2.0 on this panel is limited to 60 Hz.

S4.2 USB-C carries display, data, and 65 W of power on one cable. Suitable for the ProBook 14 (65 W) but will charge a ProBook 16 slowly, as that model expects 96 W.

S4.3 Select the input from the on-screen display, or leave auto-detect enabled.

---

## S5. On-screen display

S5.1 Press the joystick behind the lower right corner to open the menu.

S5.2 The menu reports the active input, resolution, refresh rate, and any error code.

---

## S6. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ERR-DP-0x004 | DisplayPort handshake failure | Reseat both ends. Try the supplied cable. Check for firmware update. |
| ERR-DP-0x011 | Refresh rate not supported over the current cable | Reduce to 60 Hz or use DisplayPort |
| ERR-HD-0x002 | HDMI signal out of range | Set the source output to 2560 x 1440 at 60 Hz |
| DSP-014 | Backlight failure | Service required, covered under warranty |

S6.1 ERR-DP-0x004 is the most commonly reported code on this model and is almost always a cable or seating issue rather than a panel fault. Intermittent blanking accompanied by this code should be treated as a connection problem first.

---

## S7. Firmware

S7.1 Firmware updates are downloadable from the Pacify support site and applied over USB-C from a connected computer.

S7.2 A firmware update addressing DisplayPort handshake stability was issued in revision 1.4. Panels shipped before October 2025 may benefit.

---

## S8. Care

S8.1 Clean with a dry microfibre cloth. Do not spray liquid onto the panel.

S8.2 Do not press on the panel surface. Pressure marks are physical damage and are not covered.

S8.3 Pixel defect claims follow the five-pixel threshold at POL-WAR-001 S6. Backlight and uniformity faults are covered irrespective of pixel count.
