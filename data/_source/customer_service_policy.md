S1.3 Automated assistance is available at all hours. See S12.

## S2. Response and resolution targets

| Channel | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| Live chat | 5 minutes | Same session where possible |
| Email | 24 hours | 3 business days |
| Telephone | Immediate during hours | Same call where possible |
| Complaint (S4) | 24 hours | 7 business days |
| Grievance Officer | 7 days | 30 days (statutory) |

S2.1 A first response is a substantive acknowledgement identifying the matter and the next step. An automated receipt is not a first response.

S2.2 Where a resolution target cannot be met, the customer is informed before the target expires, with a revised date and a reason.

## S3. Escalation

S3.1 Support operates three tiers.

| Tier | Handles | Escalates when |
|---|---|---|
| L1 — Support agent | Policy questions, order status, standard returns, basic troubleshooting | Matter requires a discretionary decision, or falls outside published policy |
| L2 — Specialist | Warranty adjudication, refund disputes, delay compensation, complex technical faults | Matter requires a departure from policy, or involves a legal or regulatory element |
| Grievance Officer | Unresolved complaints, statutory grievances, data protection requests | Not applicable — final internal tier |

S3.2 A customer may request escalation at any point and the request must be actioned. An agent may not decline a request to escalate.

S3.3 On escalation, the receiving tier is provided with the full interaction history, the order record, the steps already taken, and the reason for escalation. The customer is not required to restate the matter.

S3.4 Matters are escalated without a customer request where:

(a) published policy does not determine the outcome;
(b) two published policies appear to conflict;
(c) the customer has contacted Pacify three or more times about the same matter;
(d) the customer indicates an intention to pursue legal proceedings, to approach a consumer forum, or to initiate a chargeback;
(e) the matter concerns a payment discrepancy exceeding Rs 25,000;
(f) the matter concerns account access, account deletion, or a request for personal data;
(g) the customer's communication indicates significant distress.

S3.5 The grounds at S3.4 are independent. Any one is sufficient.

## S4. Complaints

S4.1 A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction with Pacify's service, as distinct from a request for information or a transaction.

S4.2 Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within **7 business days**.

S4.3 A complaint is not required to be framed as a complaint to be treated as one. Where a customer expresses dissatisfaction in the course of another enquiry, a complaint record is opened.

S4.4 The response to a complaint must address the substance of the dissatisfaction. A restatement of the policy that gave rise to the complaint is not a response.

S4.5 Where a complaint is upheld, the remedy may include correction of the underlying matter, an apology, store credit, or waiver of a fee. Where it is not upheld, the customer is given reasons and informed of the right to escalate under S3.

## S5. Grievance Officer

S5.1 In accordance with applicable consumer protection requirements, Pacify designates a Grievance Officer.

S5.2 Grievance Officer: **Ms. R. Iyer**, grievance@pacify.com. Written correspondence may be addressed to the registered office.

S5.3 The Grievance Officer responds within 7 days of receipt and disposes of the grievance within **30 days**.

S5.4 A customer is not required to exhaust S3 before approaching the Grievance Officer, but is ordinarily better served by doing so.

## S6. Identity verification

S6.1 Before any action is taken that alters an account, discloses personal data, or moves money to a destination other than the original instrument, the customer's identity must be verified.

S6.2 Verification requires **all three** of the following:

(a) a one-time password sent to the registered email address, correctly quoted;
(b) the last four digits of the payment instrument used on the account;
(c) the reference of any one order placed on the account.

S6.3 Verification under S6.2 cannot be waived, substituted, or partially satisfied. Knowledge of a customer's name, email address, telephone number, delivery address, or order contents does not constitute verification, as all of these may be known to a third party.

S6.4 Actions requiring verification under S6.1 include:

(a) changing the registered email address or telephone number;
(b) changing or adding a delivery address;
(c) disclosing order history, invoices, or payment details;
(d) supplying alternative bank details for a refund;
(e) deleting or deactivating the account;
(f) exporting personal data.

S6.5 Actions **not** requiring verification include: answering a policy question, quoting a published price, explaining a published process, and providing the status of an order where the customer already holds the order reference.

S6.6 Where verification cannot be completed in the channel in use, the matter is transferred to a verified channel. It is not deferred, worked around, or completed on the basis of partial evidence.

## S7. Account access and recovery

S7.1 Password reset is self-service through the registered email address.

S7.2 Where the registered email address is no longer accessible, recovery requires verification under S6.2, of which limb (a) is satisfied by an alternative method determined by the Grievance Officer on a case-by-case basis.

S7.3 An account may be deleted on request, subject to S6 and to S8.3.

---

## S8. Personal data

S8.1 Pacify retains order records for 7 years in accordance with tax requirements, and interaction records for 3 years.

S8.2 A customer may request a copy of their personal data. The request is fulfilled within 30 days, subject to verification under S6.

S8.3 A customer may request deletion of their personal data. Deletion is completed within 30 days, save that:

(a) order and invoice records subject to the retention period at S8.1 are retained;
(b) where a return, refund, warranty claim, or complaint is open, deletion is deferred until it is closed;
(c) records subject to a legal hold are retained.

S8.4 Where deletion is deferred under S8.3(b), the customer is told which matter is preventing deletion and when it is expected to close.

S8.5 Pacify does not sell personal data.

---

## S9. Communication preferences

S9.1 Transactional communications regarding an order, a return, or a claim cannot be opted out of while the matter is open.

S9.2 Marketing communications may be opted out of at any time from the Preferences section of the customer's Pacify account.

---

## S10. Conduct

S10.1 Pacify staff will not be abusive, dismissive, or misleading, and will not argue with a customer.

S10.2 Where a customer is abusive, uses discriminatory language, or makes a threat against an individual, the interaction may be terminated after one warning. The underlying matter continues to be dealt with in writing.

S10.3 Frustration, anger, and forceful language directed at Pacify as an organisation are not grounds for terminating an interaction.

---

## S11. Recording and monitoring

S11.1 Chat transcripts and call recordings are retained for 3 years for quality and training purposes.

S11.2 A customer may request a copy of a transcript or recording of their own interaction, subject to S6.

---

## S12. Automated assistance

S12.1 Pacify uses an automated assistant to provide first-line support at all hours.

S12.2 Where a customer asks whether they are communicating with a person or with an automated system, the answer given must be truthful and direct.

S12.3 A customer may request transfer to a human agent at any point, for any reason or none, and the request must be actioned. Where the request is made outside the hours at S1.1, a ticket is created and the customer is told when a human agent will respond.

S12.4 The automated assistant does not have authority to:

(a) approve or issue a refund;
(b) waive a fee or depart from published policy;
(c) adjudicate a warranty claim;
(d) alter an account or disclose personal data;
(e) offer compensation, discount, or goodwill credit;
(f) commit Pacify to a delivery date not confirmed by the order record.

S12.5 Matters within S12.4 are referred to a human agent. The referral carries the context gathered so far.

S12.6 The automated assistant answers from Pacify's published documentation. Where the documentation does not address a matter, or where two documents conflict, the assistant is required to say so and refer the matter rather than resolve it.
