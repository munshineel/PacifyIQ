# PACIFY ELECTRONICS PRIVATE LIMITED
## Returns Policy

**Document reference:** POL-RET-002
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective from:** 15 January 2026
**Supersedes:** POL-RET-001 (v1.0, effective 01 March 2024)
**Review cycle:** Annual
**Owner:** Customer Operations

---

## S1. Scope and application

S1.1 This Policy governs the return of goods purchased from Pacify Electronics Private Limited ("Pacify", "we", "us") through www.pacify.com or the Pacify mobile application.

S1.2 This Policy takes effect on 15 January 2026 and supersedes POL-RET-001 in its entirety. Orders placed before 15 January 2026 remain governed by POL-RET-001 for the duration of their original return window.

S1.3 This Policy is to be read together with the Refunds Policy (POL-REF-001), which governs the disbursement of monies following an approved return, and the Warranty Policy (POL-WAR-001), which governs defects arising after the expiry of the return window.

S1.4 Customers resident in the European Union are additionally subject to the EU Regional Addendum (POL-EU-001). Where the Addendum and this Policy conflict, the Addendum prevails.

S1.5 Nothing in this Policy limits any statutory right of the customer that cannot lawfully be excluded.

---

## S2. Return windows

S2.1 The applicable return window is determined by product category and by the condition of the item at the time the return is requested.

| Item category | Condition | Return window |
|---|---|---|
| Laptops, phones, tablets, monitors | Unopened, retail seal intact | 30 calendar days |
| Laptops, phones, tablets, monitors | Opened | **14 calendar days** |
| Accessories (keyboards, audio, cables, cases) | Unopened or opened | 30 calendar days |
| Bulk and business orders (5 or more units of any single SKU) | Any | 7 calendar days |
| Refurbished units | Any | 14 calendar days |

S2.2 All windows in the table at S2.1 run from the **date of delivery** as recorded by the delivery partner. The date of purchase, the date of dispatch, and the date on which the customer first uses the item are not relevant to the calculation.

S2.3 Where the final day of a return window falls on a Sunday or a gazetted public holiday in the customer's state of delivery, the window is extended to the next following business day.

S2.4 A return is treated as requested on the date the customer submits a return request through the Pacify website, the Pacify application, or a written communication to support@pacify.com. It is not necessary for the item to have been despatched within the window; only the request must fall within it.

S2.5 Return windows are not extendable at the discretion of support staff. Requests received after the window has closed must be assessed under the Warranty Policy where a defect is alleged, and are otherwise declined.

---

## S3. Meaning of "opened"

S3.1 For the purposes of S2.1, an item is "opened" where the outer retail seal has been broken, removed, cut, or otherwise compromised, whether or not the item has been removed from its packaging.

S3.2 Where a manufacturer applies a second, inner seal beneath the outer retail packaging, the condition of that inner seal does not alter the classification of the item under S3.1.

S3.3 An item whose outer retail seal is intact but whose outer shipping carton has been opened is not "opened" for the purposes of this Policy.

S3.4 Customers who open an item solely in order to inspect it for transit damage should refer to S6, which provides a separate and more favourable remedy.

---

## S4. Condition requirements

S4.1 To be eligible for return under S2, an item must be presented in the following condition:

(a) complete, including all supplied cables, adapters, documentation, and promotional inserts;
(b) free from physical damage, scratches, dents, cracks, and liquid ingress not present at delivery;
(c) bearing its original serial number label, undefaced;
(d) accompanied by its original retail packaging, in a condition permitting resale;
(e) with all accounts, activation locks, and device-level security removed or disabled;
(f) with any pre-installed licensed software unregistered.

S4.2 Where an item is delivered as part of a promotional bundle, all components of the bundle must be returned. Where a promotional item is retained, its full undiscounted retail value is deducted from the refund.

S4.3 Failure to satisfy S4.1 does not automatically void the return. Pacify may, at its discretion, accept a partially compliant return subject to a deduction reflecting the diminution in value. Any such deduction is notified to the customer before the return is processed.

---

## S5. Items that cannot be returned

S5.1 The following are not returnable under any circumstances save where they are defective:

(a) software licence keys, whether delivered physically or electronically, once the key has been revealed;
(b) gift cards and store credit vouchers once redeemed;
(c) SIM cards and eSIM activation packs once the seal is broken or the profile has been provisioned;
(d) units built to a customer's bespoke configuration specification;
(e) consumable items including printer cartridges and cleaning supplies;
(f) items marked "final sale" or "clearance - non-returnable" at the point of purchase.

S5.2 The exclusions at S5.1 do not affect the customer's rights under the Warranty Policy in respect of a manufacturing defect.

---

## S6. Items damaged or defective on arrival

S6.1 Where an item arrives visibly damaged, or is found on first use to be non-functional, the customer must report the matter to Pacify **within 48 hours of delivery**.

S6.2 A report under S6.1 must include photographs of the item, the outer packaging, and any visible damage. Where the item displays an error code, that code should be reported.

S6.3 Where a report is made within the period at S6.1 and is accepted:

(a) no restocking fee applies;
(b) return shipping is arranged and paid for by Pacify;
(c) the original outbound shipping charge, if any, is refunded;
(d) the customer may elect a replacement in lieu of a refund, subject to availability.

S6.4 Opening an item for the purpose of inspecting or documenting damage does not prejudice a claim under this section, notwithstanding S3.1.

S6.5 Where a report is made after 48 hours but within the applicable return window at S2.1, the return is processed as an ordinary return under S2, and the fees at S9 and S10 apply. The customer may in the alternative pursue a claim under the Warranty Policy.

S6.6 Where a report is made after the return window has closed, the matter is dealt with exclusively under the Warranty Policy.

---

## S7. Wrong item received

S7.1 Where the item delivered does not correspond to the item ordered, the customer should report the discrepancy within 7 calendar days of delivery.

S7.2 An accepted claim under S7.1 attracts no restocking fee and no return shipping charge, and the original outbound shipping charge is refunded.

S7.3 Pacify will despatch the correct item on receipt of the incorrect item at its warehouse, or immediately on verification where photographic evidence is conclusive.

---

## S8. Initiating a return

S8.1 Returns are initiated from the Orders section of the customer's Pacify account, or by written request to support@pacify.com quoting the order reference.

S8.2 On acceptance of a return request, Pacify issues a Return Authorisation number. Items received at the Pacify warehouse without a valid Return Authorisation number may be refused and returned to the sender at the customer's cost.

S8.3 The customer must despatch the item, or make it available for collection, within 7 calendar days of the Return Authorisation being issued. A Return Authorisation not acted upon within that period lapses.

---

## S9. Return shipping

S9.1 Responsibility for return shipping is allocated as follows:

| Reason for return | Who pays | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Change of mind | Customer | Rs 450, deducted from refund |
| Item defective on arrival (S6) | Pacify | Nil |
| Wrong item received (S7) | Pacify | Nil |
| Item not as described | Pacify | Nil |
| Bulk or business order, any reason | Customer | At cost, quoted per consignment |

S9.2 Where Pacify bears the cost of return shipping, a collection is arranged with the customer at a mutually convenient time. The customer is not required to arrange or pay for carriage.

S9.3 Where the customer bears the cost, the charge at S9.1 is a fixed contribution and is deducted from the refund calculated under the Refunds Policy. It is not payable separately at the time of despatch.

---

## S10. Restocking fees

S10.1 A restocking fee is applied to reflect the diminution in resale value of an opened item returned for reasons of customer preference.

| Item category | Condition on return | Restocking fee |
|---|---|---|
| Laptops | Opened | 10% of item value |
| Monitors | Opened | 10% of item value |
| Phones and tablets | Opened | 10% of item value |
| Accessories | Opened | Nil |
| Any category | Unopened, seal intact | Nil |

S10.2 No restocking fee is applied where the return arises from:

(a) a defect or damage reported under S6;
(b) delivery of an incorrect item under S7;
(c) a material discrepancy between the item and its published description;
(d) an exercise of statutory withdrawal rights under POL-EU-001.

S10.3 The restocking fee is calculated on the price actually paid for the item, after the application of any discount, and not on the published list price.

---

## S11. Inspection on receipt

S11.1 On receipt of a returned item at the Pacify warehouse, the item is inspected within **2 business days**.

S11.2 The inspection verifies the matters set out at S4.1 and confirms the serial number against the original order.

S11.3 Where the inspection is satisfactory, the return is approved and the file passes to the Refunds Policy for disbursement. The refund processing periods stated in that Policy begin on the date of approval, not on the date the item was despatched by the customer.

S11.4 Where the inspection identifies a shortfall against S4.1, the customer is notified within 1 business day with photographic evidence and offered the choice of:

(a) a reduced refund reflecting the diminution in value; or
(b) return of the item to the customer at the customer's cost.

S11.5 A customer who does not respond to a notification under S11.4 within 14 calendar days is deemed to have elected option (a).

---

## S12. Exchanges and replacements

S12.1 A customer within a valid return window under S2 may request an exchange for a different variant, colour, or configuration of the same product family. An exchange is treated as a return followed by a fresh purchase, and the fees at S9 and S10 apply.

S12.2 Where the exchanged item is of higher value, the difference is payable before despatch. Where it is of lower value, the difference is refunded under the Refunds Policy.

S12.3 A replacement issued under S6.3(d) is not an exchange and attracts no fee.

S12.4 The word "replacement" is used in the Warranty Policy in a distinct sense, denoting the supply of a functioning unit in satisfaction of a warranty claim outside any return window. Customers should not assume that the two are interchangeable.

---

## S13. Bulk and business orders

S13.1 An order comprising 5 or more units of a single SKU is a bulk order and is subject to a 7 calendar day return window.

S13.2 Restocking fees and return shipping charges apply to bulk orders irrespective of whether individual units have been opened.

S13.3 Partial returns of bulk orders are permitted. The restocking fee is calculated per returned unit.
