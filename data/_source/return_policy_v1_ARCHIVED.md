---

## S1. Scope

S1.1 This Policy governs the return of goods purchased from Pacify Electronics Private Limited.

S1.2 This Policy applied to orders placed between 01 March 2024 and 14 January 2026 inclusive. It has been replaced by POL-RET-002, which introduced category-specific return windows and restocking fees.

---

## S2. Return window

S2.1 **Any item purchased from Pacify may be returned within 30 days of delivery.**

S2.2 The window applies uniformly to all product categories, whether the item has been opened or not.

S2.3 The window runs from the date of delivery.

---

## S3. Condition

S3.1 Items must be returned complete, with all accessories and original packaging, and in a resaleable condition.

S3.2 Items showing damage caused after delivery may be refused.

---

## S4. Fees

S4.1 **No restocking fee is applied to any return under this Policy.**

S4.2 Return shipping is arranged and paid for by Pacify in all cases.

---

## S5. Non-returnable items

S5.1 Software keys once revealed, redeemed gift cards, and opened SIM packs are not returnable.

---

## S6. Refunds

S6.1 Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days of the item being received at the Pacify warehouse.

---

## S7. How to return

S7.1 Contact support@pacify.com quoting the order reference. A collection will be arranged.

---

*This document is retained for the purpose of administering orders placed before 15 January 2026 and for audit. For all current returns, refer to POL-RET-002.*
