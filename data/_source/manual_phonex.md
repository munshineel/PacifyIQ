## S3. Setting up

S3.1 Insert the nano-SIM using the supplied tool, or provision an eSIM from network settings.

S3.2 Note that once an eSIM profile has been provisioned, the eSIM activation pack is no longer returnable. See POL-RET-002 S5.1(c).

S3.3 Transfer data from an existing device during setup or later from settings.

---

## S4. Battery and charging

S4.1 The supplied 30 W adapter charges to about 50% in 30 minutes.

S4.2 There is no wireless charging on the Phone X. A phone placed on a wireless pad will not charge and this is not a fault.

S4.3 Battery health is in Settings, Battery, Battery health. Below 80% within 12 months is a warranty matter.

---

## S5. Water resistance

S5.1 IP53 means protection against dust and against water spray at up to 60 degrees from vertical. It is not immersion protection.

S5.2 Liquid damage is excluded from warranty cover under POL-WAR-001 S3.1(a) regardless of the IP rating. The rating describes design intent, not a guarantee.

---

## S6. Display

S6.1 The panel is OLED. Prolonged display of static content can cause image retention, which is excluded from cover as cosmetic wear under POL-WAR-001 S3.1(e).

S6.2 Dead or stuck pixel claims follow the five-pixel threshold at POL-WAR-001 S6.

---

## S7. Reset and recovery

S7.1 Force restart: hold power plus volume-down for 12 seconds.

S7.2 Factory reset from Settings, System, Reset. This erases everything.

S7.3 Recovery mode: power off, then hold volume-up while connecting the charger.

---

## S8. Care

S8.1 Use a case. The frame is aluminium and marks easily; cosmetic wear is not covered.

S8.2 Charge between 5 and 35 degrees Celsius.

S8.3 A swollen battery or a device that will not sit flat must be taken out of use immediately.
