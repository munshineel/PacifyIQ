# PACIFY ELECTRONICS PRIVATE LIMITED
## Warranty Policy

**Document reference:** POL-WAR-001
**Version:** 2.1
**Effective from:** 15 January 2026
**Owner:** Service Operations

## S1. Coverage periods

S1.1 The following warranty periods apply, running from the date of delivery.

| Product category | Brand | Warranty period |
|---|---|---|
| Laptops, phones, tablets, monitors | Pacify | 24 months |
| Laptops, phones, tablets, monitors | Third party | 12 months |
| Accessories (keyboards, audio, cases, cables) | Any | 6 months |
| Refurbished units, any category | Any | 6 months |
| Batteries as a component | Any | 12 months (see S5) |
| Power adapters and chargers | Any | 12 months |

S1.2 A warranty period is not extended by the repair or replacement of the item, save that a replaced component carries the unexpired balance of the original period or 90 days, whichever is longer.

S1.3 Warranty cover is in addition to, and does not displace, any remedy available under the Returns Policy (POL-RET-002) during the currency of a return window.

---

## S2. What is covered

S2.1 This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that manifest under normal use, including:

(a) failure of the item to power on or to hold a charge;
(b) failure of an integrated component including display, keyboard, trackpad, speakers, camera, and ports;
(c) failure of internal storage or memory;
(d) display defects meeting the threshold at S6;
(e) battery degradation meeting the threshold at S5;
(f) failure of a supplied power adapter;
(g) manufacturing faults in casing or hinges, excluding cosmetic wear.

S2.2 Cover extends to the repair of the defect, the replacement of the item, or a refund, determined in accordance with S11.

---

## S3. What is not covered

S3.1 This warranty does not cover:

(a) liquid ingress or corrosion, however caused;
(b) physical damage including cracks, dents, bends, and impact damage;
(c) damage arising from use of a non-approved charger or accessory;
(d) damage arising from unauthorised repair, disassembly, or modification;
(e) cosmetic wear including scratches, scuffs, keycap shine, and fading;
(f) software faults, operating system errors, driver conflicts, malware, and data loss;
(g) consumable components including styli tips and ear tips;
(h) failure caused by voltage irregularity, lightning, flood, fire, or other external event;
(i) items whose serial number has been removed, altered, or defaced;
(j) normal battery capacity loss not meeting the threshold at S5;
(k) issues arising from third-party software installed by the customer.

S3.2 Where a defect is found on inspection to fall within S3.1, the customer is notified and offered a paid repair quotation. Inspection in such cases attracts a fee of Rs 750, waived if the paid repair proceeds.

---

## S4. Physical damage distinguished from manufacturing defect

S4.1 The distinction between a manufacturing defect covered under S2 and physical damage excluded under S3 is a question of fact, determined on technical inspection.

S4.2 Indicators of physical damage include impact marks, deformation, cracked glass with a point of origin, and internal evidence of shock. Indicators of manufacturing defect include failure without external mark, failure of a component under no mechanical load, and a pattern of failure consistent with a known production issue.

S4.3 Where the inspection is inconclusive, the matter is referred to a Service Operations supervisor. The customer is informed of the outcome with photographic evidence and reasons.

S4.4 A defect reported within 48 hours of delivery is dealt with under POL-RET-002 S6 and not under this Policy. A defect reported after the expiry of the applicable return window is dealt with exclusively under this Policy. A defect reported after 48 hours but within the return window may be pursued under either, at the customer's election, and support staff should draw both routes to the customer's attention.

---

## S5. Battery coverage

S5.1 A battery is covered where its measured maximum capacity falls **below 80% of its rated capacity within 12 months of delivery**, under a cycle count consistent with normal use.

S5.2 Capacity is measured using the Pacify Diagnostics application or the manufacturer's equivalent utility. A reading obtained by third-party software is not accepted.

S5.3 A battery reporting error code BAT-119 has, by definition, fallen below 60% capacity and is covered if within the period at S5.1.

S5.4 Capacity loss above 80%, and capacity loss of any degree after 12 months, is normal wear and is not covered. Replacement is available as a paid service.

---

## S6. Display and pixel defects

S6.1 A display is covered where it exhibits **5 or more** dead, stuck, or permanently lit sub-pixels within the warranty period.

S6.2 A display exhibiting **fewer than 5** such pixels does not meet the threshold and is not covered. This threshold reflects panel manufacturing tolerances applied across the industry and is not subject to negotiation or to discretionary exception by support staff.

S6.3 Pixel defects are counted on inspection using a full-screen test pattern at each of black, white, red, green, and blue.

S6.4 The following display faults are covered irrespective of pixel count: backlight failure, uniform or banded discolouration, persistent flicker, visible bleed exceeding 10% of screen area, and complete panel failure. Error code DSP-014 indicates backlight failure and is covered.

S6.5 Image retention on OLED panels arising from prolonged display of static content is excluded under S3.1(e).

## S7. Commencement of the warranty period

S7.1 The warranty period runs from the **date of delivery** as recorded by the delivery partner.

S7.2 The date of purchase, the date of dispatch, the date of first power-on, and the date of account activation are not relevant to the calculation.

S7.3 Where an item is a replacement issued under this Policy, the period is calculated in accordance with S1.2.

S7.4 Powering on an item begins the practical operation of the warranty in the sense that faults become discoverable, but does not alter the commencement date at S7.1.

## S8. Pacify-branded and third-party products

S8.1 Warranty administration differs according to the brand of the item.

| Brand of item | Who administers the claim | Where the item is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Pacify | Pacify Service Operations, end to end | Pacify service centre |
| Third party (Northwind, Kestrel, and others) | The manufacturer's authorised service network | Manufacturer service centre |

S8.2 For a Pacify-branded item, the customer raises the claim with Pacify, Pacify arranges collection, and Pacify is responsible for the outcome and the turnaround time.

S8.3 For a third-party item, the warranty is given by the manufacturer and not by Pacify. Pacify's role is limited to:

(a) confirming the date of delivery and supplying proof of purchase;
(b) identifying the nearest authorised service centre;
(c) raising the matter with the manufacturer's trade channel where the customer reports a lack of response.

S8.4 Pacify cannot authorise a repair, replacement, or refund in respect of a third-party item outside the currency of a return window under POL-RET-002. Customers reporting a fault in a third-party item should be directed to S8.3 and should not be given an expectation of a Pacify-administered remedy.

S8.5 The distinction at S8.1 turns on the brand of the item and not on the identity of the seller. All items sold on www.pacify.com are sold by Pacify; not all are Pacify-branded.

## S9. PacifyCare+ extended warranty

S9.1 PacifyCare+ extends the applicable period at S1.1 by a further 12 or 24 months.

S9.2 PacifyCare+ must be purchased **within 30 calendar days of delivery** of the covered item. It cannot be purchased later, and cannot be purchased in respect of an item with a subsisting unresolved fault.

S9.3 PacifyCare+ extends the period only. It does not widen the scope of cover, and in particular does not cover any matter excluded under S3.1. It is not accidental damage insurance.

S9.4 PacifyCare+ is refundable pro rata on the return of the covered item, and is transferable with the item under S14.

## S10. Making a claim

S10.1 Claims are raised through the customer's Pacify account, or by written request to support@pacify.com quoting the order reference.

S10.2 The following are required:

(a) the order reference and item serial number;
(b) a description of the fault and the date it was first observed;
(c) any error code displayed;
(d) photographs or a short video demonstrating the fault, where the fault is observable;
(e) for battery claims, a capacity reading obtained under S5.2.

S10.3 Where troubleshooting steps in the Technical Support FAQ have been attempted, the customer should say which. This shortens diagnosis and is not a precondition of the claim.

S10.4 A claim is acknowledged within 1 business day and an inspection outcome is issued within 3 business days of receipt of the item.

---

## S11. Repair, replacement, or refund

S11.1 Where a claim is accepted, the remedy is determined in the following order:

(a) **repair**, where the fault is repairable and repair restores the item to specification;
(b) **replacement**, where repair is not viable, with an item of the same model or, if unavailable, of equivalent or superior specification;
(c) **refund**, where neither repair nor replacement is possible.

S11.2 A refund under S11.1(c) is of the price originally paid for the item and is disbursed under POL-REF-001. It is not adjusted for depreciation or for the period of use.

S11.3 The customer does not have a right to elect between the remedies at S11.1. The election is Pacify's, exercised reasonably.

S11.4 A replacement issued under S11.1(b) is not an "exchange" within the meaning of POL-RET-002 S12 and attracts no restocking fee and no shipping charge.

---

## S12. Turnaround

S12.1 The target turnaround for a warranty repair is **7 to 14 business days** from receipt of the item at the service centre.

S12.2 Where a part must be imported, the turnaround may extend to 21 business days. The customer is notified where this arises.

S12.3 Where turnaround exceeds 30 business days, the customer may require a replacement under S11.1(b) or, if unavailable, a refund under S11.1(c).

S12.4 A loan unit is not provided as a matter of course. Loan units may be offered at Pacify's discretion for Pacify-branded items where turnaround is expected to exceed 14 business days.

---

## S13. Voiding

S13.1 Warranty cover is void where:

(a) the item has been opened, disassembled, or repaired by a person not authorised by Pacify or by the manufacturer;
(b) the serial number has been removed, altered, or rendered illegible;
(c) a security seal internal to the item has been broken;
(d) the item has been used in a commercial rental, mining, or continuous-load application not contemplated by its specification.

S13.2 The installation of software, including a different operating system, does not void hardware warranty cover. Faults arising from such software are excluded under S3.1(f) but the hardware remains covered.

---

## S14. Transfer on resale

S14.1 Warranty cover attaches to the item and transfers to a subsequent owner for the unexpired balance of the period.

S14.2 The subsequent owner must produce the original invoice. Pacify does not update account ownership records and claims continue to be logged against the original order reference.
