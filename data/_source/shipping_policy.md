# PACIFY ELECTRONICS PRIVATE LIMITED
## Shipping and Delivery Policy

**Document reference:** POL-SHP-001
**Version:** 1.7
**Effective from:** 15 January 2026
**Owner:** Logistics

---

## S1. Serviceable regions

S1.1 Pacify delivers to all serviceable pincodes within India. Serviceability is confirmed at checkout on entry of the delivery pincode.

S1.2 Pacify delivers to the following European Union member states: Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, and Sweden.

S1.3 Pacify does not deliver outside the territories at S1.1 and S1.2. Orders placed for delivery elsewhere are cancelled and refunded in full.

S1.4 Certain pincodes are subject to restricted service, being limited to standard shipping and to prepaid orders only. Restricted pincodes include remote and border districts and are identified at checkout.

---

## S2. Shipping methods and timelines

| Method | Transit time | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-7 business days | All serviceable pincodes |
| Express | 1-2 business days | Metro and tier-1 pincodes |
| White glove | 5-10 business days | Monitors of 32 inches and above |
| EU standard | 6-12 business days | EU member states at S1.2 |

S2.1 Transit times are counted in business days and exclude Sundays and gazetted public holidays.

S2.2 Transit time is measured from **dispatch**, not from the placing of the order. The period before dispatch is dealt with at S4.

S2.3 Transit times are estimates based on carrier performance and are not guaranteed. Committed delivery dates, where given at checkout, are addressed at S8.

---

## S3. Shipping charges

| Order value | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| Below Rs 5,000 | Rs 99 | Rs 299 |
| Rs 5,000 and above | Free | Rs 299 |

S3.1 The free shipping threshold is applied to the order value after discounts and before taxes.

S3.2 Where an order qualifying for free shipping is partially returned such that the retained value falls below the threshold, the shipping charge that would have applied is recovered from the refund. See POL-REF-001 S5.2.

S3.3 White glove delivery is charged at Rs 899 irrespective of order value.

S3.4 EU orders are charged EUR 12 for standard delivery, free above EUR 400. Customs duties are addressed at S9.

---

## S4. Order processing

S4.1 Orders are processed and handed to the carrier within **24 hours** of payment confirmation, being 1 business day.

S4.2 Orders placed after 18:00 IST, or on a Sunday or public holiday, are processed on the next business day.

S4.3 Processing time is separate from and additional to the transit times at S2. A standard order placed on a Monday morning is therefore ordinarily delivered between the following Thursday and the following Wednesday.

S4.4 Orders for items shown as "made to order" or "pre-order" are processed on the date stated on the product page.

S4.5 Payment confirmation for cash on delivery orders occurs on order placement. Payment confirmation for prepaid orders occurs on successful settlement, which may lag the order by up to 2 hours.

---

## S5. Tracking

S5.1 A tracking reference is issued by email and SMS on dispatch, and is visible in the Orders section of the customer's Pacify account.

S5.2 Tracking information is supplied by the carrier and may lag physical movement by up to 12 hours.

S5.3 A consignment showing no movement for more than 5 consecutive business days should be reported to Pacify, which will raise a trace with the carrier. A trace outcome is ordinarily available within 3 business days.

---

## S6. Address changes

S6.1 A delivery address may be changed at any time **before dispatch**, from the Orders section of the customer's Pacify account.

S6.2 After dispatch, the address cannot be changed. Pacify has no facility to redirect a consignment in transit.

S6.3 A customer who requires delivery to a different address after dispatch must decline the delivery, allow the consignment to return to origin, and place a fresh order. No charge arises for the declined delivery.

S6.4 Requests to change the address after dispatch are declined at first line and are not a matter for escalation.

---

## S7. Failed delivery

S7.1 The carrier makes up to **3 delivery attempts** on consecutive business days.

S7.2 Where all 3 attempts fail, the consignment is returned to origin. On receipt, the order is cancelled and refunded under POL-REF-001, less the outbound shipping charge where one was levied.

S7.3 Where a delivery fails because the recipient was unavailable, no charge arises for the additional attempts.

S7.4 Where a delivery fails because the address was incorrect or incomplete as supplied by the customer, and a fresh order is placed, shipping is charged afresh.

S7.5 Prepaid orders returned to origin under S7.2 are refunded within the periods at POL-REF-001 S3.

---

## S8. Delays and compensation

S8.1 Where delivery is not effected within **5 business days** of a committed delivery date given at checkout, the customer may claim compensation.

S8.2 Compensation takes the form of store credit of Rs 500, or the value of the shipping charge paid, whichever is greater.

S8.3 Compensation is not payable where the delay arises from:

(a) an incorrect or incomplete address supplied by the customer;
(b) the recipient being unavailable at the times attempted;
(c) a natural event, civil disturbance, strike, or governmental restriction;
(d) a delay in customs clearance not attributable to Pacify's documentation.

S8.4 Where a delay exceeds 15 business days beyond the committed date, the customer may cancel the order for a full refund including all shipping charges, whether or not the consignment has been dispatched.

S8.5 Claims under this section are made to support@pacify.com and are determined within 3 business days.

---

## S9. Customs and duties on EU orders

S9.1 Orders to the European Union are shipped on a delivered-at-place basis. Import duties, value added tax, and customs handling charges are payable by the recipient.

S9.2 Pacify supplies a commercial invoice stating the true transaction value. Pacify does not under-declare consignment values.

S9.3 Where a consignment is refused at customs and returned to origin, the order is refunded less outbound shipping and less any customs charges levied against Pacify.

S9.4 Delays in customs clearance do not count towards the periods at S8.1.

---

## S10. White glove delivery

S10.1 Monitors of 32 inches and above are delivered on a white glove basis, comprising two-person delivery, placement in the room of the customer's choice, unboxing, and removal of packaging.

S10.2 White glove deliveries are scheduled by appointment. The customer is contacted within 2 business days of dispatch to agree a slot.

S10.3 White glove delivery does not include mounting, wall fixing, or cable management.

S10.4 A missed white glove appointment attracts a rescheduling charge of Rs 400.
